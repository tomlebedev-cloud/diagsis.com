About us

A reliable partner for Lithuanian laboratories

UAB Diagnostinės sistemos is a Lithuanian company that has supplied hospitals, clinics and laboratories with modern diagnostic equipment, reagents and solutions since 1994.

Our goal is to keep laboratories running accurately, quickly and without interruption, from choosing the right solution to installation and long-term technical service.

Expertise

What we specialise in

We work in the key areas of laboratory diagnostics, where accuracy and reliability matter most.

Coagulation testing

Haemostasis, platelet function and thromboelastometry solutions for clinical laboratories.

Immunology testing

Equipment and reagents for immunochemistry, specialist proteins and rapid tests.

Genetic testing

Molecular diagnostics and FISH technologies for modern laboratories.

Clinical chemistry

Biochemistry analysers, reagents, calibrators and quality control materials.

How we work

From your needs to a working laboratory

  1. Needs assessment

    We learn your test volumes, laboratory workflow and requirements.

  2. Choosing a solution

    We propose the right equipment and reagents from trusted manufacturers.

  3. Delivery and installation

    We deliver, install and prepare the equipment for routine work.

  4. Maintenance and service

    We provide technical maintenance and fast repairs.
