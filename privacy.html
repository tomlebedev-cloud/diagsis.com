3. Cookies and third-party content

This website does not use cookies, visitor analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are served from our own server, so no third parties are contacted to load them.

The map in the Contact section loads only after you click “Show map”. Your browser then connects to Google servers, and Google Ireland Limited receives your IP address and may set cookies. This is governed by Google's privacy policy.

The fault registration system is a separate application. It may use strictly necessary session cookies for signing in.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only as far as necessary:

  • with equipment and reagent manufacturers, for warranty service, fault investigation or legally required incident reporting;
  • with providers of IT, email, hosting and accounting services, under data processing agreements;
  • with public authorities, where required by law.

Some manufacturers operate outside the European Economic Area, for example in the United States and the United Kingdom. In that case we transfer data only where there is a European Commission adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses apply.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased or its processing restricted;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • receive the data you provided in a commonly used machine-readable format.

Send your request to arnonas@diagsis.com. We will reply within one month and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

If you believe your data is processed unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the State Data Protection Inspectorate of Lithuania: L. Sapiegos g. 17, LT-10312 Vilnius, ada@ada.lt, vdai.lrv.lt.

6. How we protect data

Only employees who need the data for their work can access it. We use access control, encrypted connections and backups, and we review our security measures regularly.

7. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our data processing changes. The latest version is always published on this page, with its date shown at the top.
